# Privacy Policy for CP Devotional

**Effective date:** August 8, 2026
**Last updated:** August 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CP Devotional ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application.

By using CP Devotional, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you create an account, we collect:

- **Email address** — used to create and secure your account, and to log you in.
- **Password** — stored only as a securely hashed value; we never store or have access to your plain-text password.
- **Display name** — shown to other users where applicable (e.g., public profile).
- **Bio** (optional) — a short free-text description you may choose to add to your profile.

When you use certain features of the App, we collect the following:

- **Ratings/reactions** — if you rate a devotion, testimony, or song (1–5 stars), the rating is stored **without any identifying information** linking it back to your account.

### 1.2 App Content You Interact With

The App currently offers three features, none of which require you to submit personal content:

- **Daily Devotions** — devotional readings and reflections curated and displayed to you. You do not submit content for this feature.
- **Testimonies** — written testimony stories curated and displayed to you. You do not submit content for this feature.
- **Music Ministration** — worship and praise audio tracks that stream from our servers for in-app playback. Playing a track does not send any personal information to a third party; the audio file is served directly by our backend.

Other features referenced elsewhere in the App (such as Prayer and Prophetic Utterances) are placeholders that are not yet implemented and do not collect or process any data at this time. This policy will be updated before those features are released.

### 1.3 Information We Do Not Collect

CP Devotional does **not** collect:

- Precise location data
- Contacts, photos, or media from your device
- Payment or financial information
- Advertising identifiers
- Push notification tokens
- Analytics, crash, or usage-tracking data of any kind (the App does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs)

### 1.4 Automatically Collected Information

We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs. Our backend servers may log standard technical information (such as IP address and request timestamps) as part of normal server operation, for security and troubleshooting purposes only. This information is not used to build advertising profiles and is not shared with third parties.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect only to:

- Create and authenticate your account
- Provide access to the App's features (Daily Devotions, Testimonies, and Music Ministration)
- Display your display name and bio on your profile, where applicable
- Maintain the security and integrity of the App
- Respond to support requests you send us

We do **not** sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

## 3. How Your Information Is Stored and Secured

- Your password is hashed using industry-standard cryptographic hashing (bcrypt) before storage — we cannot recover your original password.
- Your login session is managed using a signed authentication token, stored securely on your device.
- Your data is stored in a private database that is not publicly accessible.
- We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 4. Third-Party Services

CP Devotional's backend uses the following third-party services to generate content **on our servers**. These providers do not receive your account credentials, email address, or personal profile information:

- **Anthropic (Claude API)** — used to help generate devotional and testimony written content.
- **ElevenLabs** — used to generate spoken-word devotional audio and the music tracks used in Music Ministration.
- **Bible API (bible-api.com)** — used to retrieve Bible verse text.

These services process content generation requests on our behalf and do not receive your personal account information.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account remains active. If you would like your account and associated personal data deleted, you may request deletion at any time (see Section 8). Anonymous content you submitted (such as ratings), which is not linked to your account, may continue to exist in the App after account deletion, as it cannot be tied back to you.

## 6. Children's Privacy

CP Devotional is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the information below and we will take steps to delete such information.

## 7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

- Access the personal information we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate personal information (e.g., by updating your name or bio in the App)
- Request deletion of your account and associated personal information
- Withdraw consent to processing, where applicable

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information in Section 8.
