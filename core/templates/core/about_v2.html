The long way round

Four chapters, one thread.

  1. 01

    Engineering foundations

    I studied Chemical Engineering at the Federal University of Technology in Nigeria, where I was elected President of the Chemical Engineering Society (Student Chapter) and first started writing code. After graduating I spent a year in project management at Shell Nigeria Gas Ltd — a short stint that set the tone for everything that followed.

  2. 02

    Operations, computerised

    I moved into the distribution sector and led the company's move to computerised operations, run as a project from day one. Streamlining those processes earned me the role of Business Operations Manager, which I held for three years — blending technical skill with the day-to-day realities of running a business.

  3. 03

    Founder

    In 2009 I founded my own company, investing in and redistributing alcoholic beverages through a demanding, regulated market. In 2015 I co-founded and led a second business. Those years taught me what software has to get right when real money, real stock and real people depend on it.

  4. 04

    Technology, full-time

    Today I build the systems I once wished I had: SCusH, an operations platform in production since 2021; Networth, a multi-currency financial tracker with an AI advisor; and CP Devotional, a public app on Google Play. I graduated with honours from Nova Scotia Community College's Advanced Certificate in IT Programming with AI, and I've deepened my project-management practice through Google's career certificate programme.

“I'm drawn to the business case — the place where technology and business sense meet to create something people can actually use.”
