The person behind the work

An engineer. A builder. A problem-solver first.

I trained as a chemical engineer, which taught me to think in processes, controls and feedback loops. Today I apply that same discipline to software — building practical systems that solve real business problems, from payroll and warehousing to personal finance.

I work across Python, Django, FastAPI and modern AI tooling to move ideas from concept to production, and I care most about the things that make software trustworthy: clean data, audited records and decisions you can explain.
